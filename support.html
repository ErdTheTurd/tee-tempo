FAQ

Do I need an iPhone app?

No. Tee Tempo is watch-only. The App Store listing installs a small container so the watch app can be delivered; there is no iPhone companion to open or configure.

How do the taps work?

Each swing has three haptics: takeaway (start of the backswing), the top, and a firmer impact tap. The default rhythm is 3:1 — backswing about three times as long as the downswing.

What are Smooth, Tour, and Quick?

Named paces at a 3:1 ratio. Smooth is 1.07 seconds total, Tour is 0.93 seconds (the default), and Quick is 0.80 seconds. Custom lets you set ratio (2:1–5:1), pace (0.70–1.40s), and the pause between swings (0.6–3.0s). Turn the Digital Crown to nudge pace.

What are Ladder and Steady?

Ladder runs five swings at Smooth, then Tour, then Quick. Steady repeats your current pace for 5, 10, 15, or 20 swings with a soft three-tap countdown before each swing. Both end with a short session summary.

Where is my swing history stored?

Only on your Apple Watch. Tee Tempo does not sync history to iCloud or a server, and nothing is sent to us. Delete the app and the log goes with it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Practice, drills, History, and complications work without a network connection. There is no account to sign into.

Can I start from Siri or a complication?

Yes. Use the Start Tee Tempo shortcut, or add a Tee Tempo complication that shows your current preset and pace.

I feel weak or missing taps in the Simulator

Real haptic feedback needs a physical Apple Watch. The Simulator can show the UI timing, but the taps themselves are meant for the wrist.
