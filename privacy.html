Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Swingtick is a watch-only Apple Watch app published by Edward Dunn. Golf is available today. Tennis and other swing sports are planned later and are not in the app yet.

We do not collect data

Swingtick does not collect personal data. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send information to us or to any third party.

What stays on your watch

Your swing history, presets, and settings are stored on your Apple Watch only. Nothing in that log leaves the device. If you delete the app, that on-device data is removed with it.

Offline by design

Swingtick works without a network connection. Practice, drills, History, and complications do not require the internet.

No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. There are no ads and no third-party measurement tools in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: erdunn706@gmail.com.
