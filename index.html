Coming soon for Apple Watch. Free. Works offline.

Three taps. One ratio.

The backswing takes about three times as long as the downswing. Swingtick puts that pace on your wrist so you feel it instead of watching a clock.

Pick a pace. Turn the Crown when you need something else.

Smooth (1.07s), Tour (0.93s), and Quick (0.80s) cover most sessions. Custom opens ratio from 2:1 to 5:1, pace from 0.70s to 1.40s, and pause from 0.6s to 3.0s. Roll the Digital Crown on the range without leaving the Ready screen.

  • Smooth 1.07s
  • Tour 0.93s
  • Quick 0.80s
  • Custom your numbers

Ladder shifts the pace. Steady holds it.

Ladder runs five swings at Smooth, then Tour, then Quick. Steady locks one pace for 5, 10, 15, or 20 swings, with a soft three-tap countdown before each one. When you finish, the watch shows what you completed and how long you were out.

Watch-only. Offline. History stays put.

There is no iPhone app to open. Swingtick lives on Apple Watch: complications for your current pace, a Start Swingtick shortcut for Siri, and a History chart between sessions. Nothing leaves the watch. No account. No analytics.
