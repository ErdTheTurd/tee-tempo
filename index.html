Swingtick

A watch app that helps you swing smoother.

I kept rushing my swing, so I made an app that taps my wrist to keep my timing even. It's for golf right now, and I'm adding tennis next.

Download on theApp Store

Coming soon to Apple Watch. It's free, and you don't need your phone with you.

How it works

Your watch taps once when you start your backswing, once at the top, and once when you should hit the ball. A lot of good golfers take about three times longer going back than coming down, so that's the timing it uses.

Faster or slower

There are three speeds to pick from. If none of them feel right, you can set your own. You can also turn the crown on the side of your watch to speed it up or slow it down.

  • Smooth 1.07s
  • Tour 0.93s
  • Quick 0.80s
  • Custom your numbers

Practice

You can do a set of swings at one speed, or start slow and work up to fast. It counts you in before each swing.

You don't need your phone

Everything is on the watch, so you can leave your phone in the bag. It keeps track of your practice, and nothing gets sent anywhere. You don't have to sign up for anything.
