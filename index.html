The feel

Takeaway. Top. Impact.

Tour players take the club back unhurried, then come down in about one-third the time. Tee Tempo trains that 3:1 ratio on your wrist.

A tap starts the backswing. A second tap marks the top. Impact is the firmer tap — when the club should meet the ball.

Pace

Pick a preset, or turn the Crown.

Smooth (1.07s), Tour (0.93s, the default), and Quick (0.80s) cover most practice days. Need something in between? Dig into Custom — ratio from 2:1 to 5:1, pace from 0.70s to 1.40s, pause from 0.6s to 3.0s. The Digital Crown rolls the pace while you stay on the range.

Drills

Ladder up. Hold Steady.

Ladder runs five swings each at Smooth, then Tour, then Quick — so your body learns the shift without you counting.

Steady locks one pace for 5, 10, 15, or 20 swings, with a soft three-tap countdown before each one. When you finish, the watch shows what you completed and how long you were out there.

On the wrist

Watch-only. Offline. Your log, your watch.

There is no iPhone app to babysit. Tee Tempo lives on Apple Watch — complications for your current pace, a Start Tee Tempo shortcut for Siri, and History charts you can check between buckets.

Nothing leaves the watch. No account. No analytics. Raise your wrist, pick Tour, and feel the three taps.
